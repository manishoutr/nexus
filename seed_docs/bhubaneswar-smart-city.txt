Bhubaneswar Smart City Mission

Bhubaneswar, the capital of Odisha, was ranked first among the twenty
lighthouse cities selected in the first round of India's Smart Cities
Mission in 2016. The mission aimed to improve urban infrastructure, promote
sustainable transport and use technology to deliver public services.

Key Projects
The city introduced a common payment card called the Odyssey card for its
Mo Bus public transport service. The Mo Bus network connects Bhubaneswar
with the neighbouring cities of Cuttack and Khordha. Public bicycle sharing
under the Mo Cycle programme was launched to encourage short trips without
motor vehicles.

Technology Initiatives
The Bhubaneswar Smart City Limited special-purpose vehicle set up an
integrated command and control centre. The centre monitors traffic cameras,
manages adaptive traffic signals and coordinates emergency response. Free
public Wi-Fi hotspots were installed in busy market areas and along major
roads.

Citizen Engagement
The mission ran a citizen engagement programme called the Bhubaneswar Town
Centre District project, which redesigned public spaces around Master
Canteen Square. Residents participated through surveys and design workshops
to choose how streets and squares were rebuilt.

Challenges
Observers noted that maintaining smart infrastructure after mission funding
ends is a challenge. Integrating services across city, state and central
agencies requires sustained coordination and clear ownership of data.
