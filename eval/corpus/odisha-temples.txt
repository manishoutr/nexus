Temples of Odisha

Odisha is known for three temple clusters. Puri has the Jagannath temple, which is the centre of the yearly Rath Yatra, when the three deities are pulled through the main avenue on large wooden chariots. Konark has the Sun Temple, built in the thirteenth century and designed as a giant chariot with twelve pairs of carved stone wheels. Bhubaneswar has dozens of older temples around Bindu Sagar lake, and the Lingaraj temple is the largest of them.

The Kalinga style of architecture has three parts. The deul is the tower over the sanctum, the jagamohana is the porch where worshippers gather, and the nata mandira is a hall for ritual dance. Sandstone and laterite were the usual materials.

Visitors are asked to dress modestly. Some temples admit only Hindus to the inner sanctum, while the outer grounds are open to everyone.
